Find Revenue Leakage in Discount Approvals With Automation
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Discounts can help close the right deals, yet approval practices often vary by seller, product, and region. When approvals sit in email or chat, finance and sales leaders struggle to see whether exceptions are consistent with pricing strategy.

An automation can collect discount requests in a standard format, calculate margin context, apply approval thresholds, and record the final rationale. AI can summarize patterns such as repeated objections, unusual combinations, or requests that may point to a packaging problem.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Unmanaged discounting creates revenue leakage and makes forecasting less reliable. A disciplined workflow does not eliminate commercial judgment; it gives that judgment better information and a traceable record.

It can also shorten approval time for routine requests.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Define guardrails by product, segment, term length, and margin floor. Send within-policy requests through a fast path and route exceptions with deal context to the right approver. Review monthly trends by rep, product, and reason, then adjust enablement or pricing where the same exception repeats.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

Discount controls are most useful when they help teams learn, not merely say no. Automate the evidence and routing so leaders can protect margin while keeping good deals moving.
