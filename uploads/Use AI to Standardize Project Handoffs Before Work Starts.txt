Use AI to Standardize Project Handoffs Before Work Starts
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Projects often stumble before the kickoff meeting. Sales, implementation, and delivery teams each hold part of the story: promised scope, customer goals, technical constraints, stakeholders, timing, and risks. When that information moves by scattered notes and forwarded emails, the delivery team begins by reconstructing the deal.

AI can turn approved deal records, call notes, and proposal documents into a structured handoff brief. Automation can then check whether required fields are complete, create the kickoff workspace, assign owners, and request clarification before the project is accepted.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Poor handoffs cause scope disputes, delayed starts, and frustrated customers who have to repeat themselves. They also distort delivery capacity because teams discover complexity after staffing decisions have already been made.

A consistent handoff creates a shared version of the starting point. It gives project leaders a chance to challenge assumptions early, when changes are less expensive and customer expectations are easier to reset.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Treat the workflow as a readiness check, not a document generator.

• Extract objectives, deliverables, exclusions, and commercial commitments.
• Identify missing owners, dates, integrations, or customer dependencies.
• Create a concise brief linked to the source records.
• Route the brief to sales and delivery for explicit acceptance.
• Open the project only after critical gaps have a named resolution owner.

Monitor rework in the first thirty days, time to kickoff, scope changes, and the number of projects accepted with unresolved dependencies.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

Automation cannot eliminate project uncertainty, but it can make uncertainty visible before work starts. A disciplined handoff workflow protects the customer experience and gives delivery teams a more reliable foundation.
