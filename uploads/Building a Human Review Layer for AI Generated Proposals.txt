Building a Human Review Layer for AI Generated Proposals
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Proposal teams spend significant time locating approved language, adapting past material, and checking that a response matches the buyer's request. AI can accelerate the first draft, but a draft that sounds confident can still contain an outdated capability, unsupported promise, or missed requirement.

A strong workflow retrieves approved content, creates a traceable draft, and sends it through a role-based review. Sales checks customer fit, delivery checks scope, legal checks terms, and an executive owner approves final commitments. The automation records the evidence used and the changes made.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Faster proposal production is useful only when accuracy survives. A review layer protects margin and credibility while allowing teams to respond without rebuilding standard material from scratch.

Measure draft preparation time, review-cycle time, the rate of unsupported claims found before submission, win rate by proposal type, and post-sale scope changes.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Create a curated source library with owners and expiration dates. Restrict the drafting workflow to that library, and require reviewer signoff for pricing, security statements, implementation timelines, and any customer-specific commitment.

Use reviewer edits as feedback. If the same correction appears repeatedly, improve the source content or the drafting instructions.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

AI can make the blank page disappear. A well-designed human review layer ensures the resulting proposal is not only fast, but trustworthy and commercially sound.
