Keeping Internal Policy Answers Current With AI Content Checks
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Employees frequently ask the same questions about expenses, travel, security, benefits, and operating procedures. Internal help articles can become stale when a policy changes, leaving teams to rely on old links or informal advice. AI can compare drafts and published answers with a controlled policy source, flag inconsistencies, and identify pages likely to need an owner review.

This is a content-maintenance workflow, not a replacement for policy authority. The approved source and accountable owner must remain clear.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Outdated internal guidance wastes time and can lead to inconsistent decisions. A recurring check is more practical than waiting for a surge of questions to reveal a problem.

It can help organizations:
- Find conflicting instructions across knowledge channels
- Prioritize articles with high usage and high change risk
- Reduce repetitive employee support requests
- Maintain a visible review history for key policies

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Maintain a versioned source of truth for each policy. Schedule AI checks after an approved policy update and at regular intervals for high-traffic articles. Ask it to report the exact sentences that appear inconsistent, the policy version it used, and the content owner who should decide the update.

Measure stale-content findings, review completion time, and the rate at which employees still escalate answered questions. Do not publish machine rewrites without owner approval, especially for legal, financial, or people-related guidance.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

The value of AI in a knowledge base is not simply faster answers. It is a disciplined way to notice when the answer employees see no longer matches the rule they are expected to follow.
