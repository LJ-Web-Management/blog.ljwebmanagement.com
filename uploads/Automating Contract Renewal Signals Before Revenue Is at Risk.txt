Automating Contract Renewal Signals Before Revenue Is at Risk
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Renewal risk rarely appears on the contract end date. It builds through low product usage, unresolved service issues, incomplete executive relationships, and missed notice periods. When those signals live in separate systems, account teams discover them too late.

Automation can consolidate renewal dates, support activity, usage indicators, and account tasks into a focused review list. AI can summarize the recent account story and draft a preparation checklist, while the account owner decides what action is appropriate.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Retention work becomes more predictable when teams can see the same leading signals. It also reduces dependence on individual memory during account transitions.

Common improvements include:

• Earlier outreach for contracts with notice deadlines
• Clearer ownership of renewal preparation
• More consistent risk notes for leadership reviews

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Define a small set of signals with accountable data owners. For example, create a review task 120 days before renewal, escalate unresolved critical cases, and flag accounts with no recent meaningful contact. Do not allow a generic score to replace the owner’s written assessment.

Measure renewal preparation lead time, task completion, and the accuracy of risk flags over time. The goal is to improve the quality of conversations, not simply increase alerts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

Contract renewal automation works best as an early-warning system. Gather the evidence early, present it in context, and give people time to build the right customer plan.
