From Knowledge Base to Guided Employee Workflow
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Most organizations have useful policies and procedures, but employees often cannot find the right passage at the moment of work. A traditional knowledge base answers a search query. A guided workflow uses approved knowledge to help a person complete a specific task with the required steps, forms, and escalation points.

For a new manager handling a leave request, the system can identify the applicable policy, ask the necessary factual questions, provide the current form, and direct exceptions to HR. It should not make an employment decision or hide the underlying policy from the manager.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Guidance at the point of work reduces inconsistency and repeat questions. It can also protect specialists from spending their day answering routine process questions that a well-designed workflow could resolve.

Track time to complete the task, policy-search time, escalation rate, completion errors, and employee satisfaction with the guidance.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Select one high-volume internal process with stable rules. Assign content owners, revision dates, and a process for withdrawing outdated material. Present citations or links to the approved source within the workflow, and require a human escalation for exceptions or sensitive cases.

Test with real employees before broad rollout. Their questions will reveal ambiguity that a document review alone misses.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

The next step after a searchable knowledge base is not a more eloquent chatbot. It is a governed workflow that helps people act correctly, with clear sources and clear boundaries.
