Automating Expense Policy Checks Before Reimbursement Delays
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Expense reports tend to create friction at the worst possible time: after an employee has spent money for work and before finance has enough information to reimburse it. Receipts are incomplete, categories are inconsistent, and approvers receive a batch of reports with no explanation of what needs attention. AI-assisted extraction can turn those documents into structured, reviewable records before they enter the approval queue.

The objective is not to let a model decide whether a charge is legitimate. It is to surface missing evidence and possible policy conflicts early, with a clear link back to the receipt and policy.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Late reimbursement damages employee trust, while weak checks create compliance exposure. Manual review also makes policy enforcement uneven across teams and geographies.

When exceptions are categorized consistently, finance can improve the policy itself instead of repeatedly explaining it one report at a time.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Use the workflow to prepare, not replace, control points:

- Read receipts and suggest merchant, date, amount, currency, and expense category.
- Compare fields with approved limits, required attendees, and documentation rules.
- Ask the employee for a missing business purpose before routing to an approver.
- Send only genuine exceptions to finance, along with the applicable rule and source evidence.

Review accuracy by category before expanding the system. Measure reimbursement cycle time, exception rate, manual touches per report, and the most common preventable errors.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

Expense automation earns trust when it gives employees immediate, specific guidance and gives finance better evidence. Keep policies deterministic, make the AI output explainable, and use the results to simplify the process over time.
