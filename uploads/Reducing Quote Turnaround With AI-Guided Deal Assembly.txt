Reducing Quote Turnaround With AI-Guided Deal Assembly
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Complex quotes often require sales, operations, finance, and product teams to assemble pricing, service terms, configurations, and approvals. The delay rarely comes from typing the document. It comes from missing information and uncertainty about which precedent applies. AI can summarize the deal request, identify required inputs, and guide the seller toward approved components.

The workflow should retrieve current catalog and pricing data from source systems. It must not create unapproved discounts or infer contractual terms.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

Quote speed affects win rate, but inaccurate quotes create rework and margin loss. A guided process makes the commercial path faster while protecting the controls that keep a deal healthy.

Potential gains include:
- Fewer back-and-forth messages for missing details
- More consistent use of approved packages
- Earlier visibility into nonstandard requests
- Better tracking of approval bottlenecks

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Begin with a high-volume offer that has defined pricing and common options. Have the assistant create a deal brief, ask only the necessary follow-up questions, and assemble a quote draft from approved components. Route discount, scope, and term exceptions based on thresholds that leaders can explain.

Track time to first complete quote, revisions after approval, discount leakage, and conversion by quote type. Use those metrics to improve the process rather than grading individual sellers on machine suggestions.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

AI can make quoting feel less like a scavenger hunt. The business value comes from connecting a faster draft to current data, explicit approvals, and a visible exception path.
