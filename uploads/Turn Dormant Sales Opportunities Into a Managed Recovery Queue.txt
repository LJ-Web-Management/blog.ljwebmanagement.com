Turn Dormant Sales Opportunities Into a Managed Recovery Queue
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
What Happened

Sales pipelines rarely lose momentum all at once. Opportunities become dormant when a next step is missing, an email has no reply, a proposal waits too long, or a promised date passes. These signals are often available in the CRM but are difficult for managers to review consistently.

An AI-assisted workflow can examine activity history and create a recovery queue. Rather than sending generic reminders, it groups opportunities by likely reason for delay and suggests a practical next action for the account owner.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Why It Matters for Businesses

A dormant opportunity is not automatically lost, but it does need a decision. A shared queue improves forecast quality and prevents revenue from depending on an individual rep’s memory.

It also helps leadership distinguish healthy long-cycle deals from records that should be requalified or closed.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The Practical Automation Opportunity

Set clear inactivity rules by deal stage. For each flagged record, assemble the last customer interaction, open tasks, deal value, and a recommended choice: contact, escalate, revise, nurture, or close. Require the owner to select an outcome, then track recovery rate by segment and stage.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Business Takeaway

Pipeline hygiene improves when dormant deals become a visible operating queue. Use AI to surface the context, while salespeople retain responsibility for the relationship decision.
